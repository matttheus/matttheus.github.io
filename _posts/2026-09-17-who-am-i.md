Hey, what's up? My name is Matheus, I'm a software engineer, and this is the
first post on this blog. Here I'll introduce myself, tell a bit of my story, and
share my goals with this blog.

This blog also has a companion: my
[YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@{{ site.youtube_handle }}). Every
subject I write about here will also show up there as a video, so you can pick
the format you prefer: read it here or watch it there.

I'm not a native English speaker. I'm from Brazil, and I'm
writing in English for two reasons. First, I want to reach people all around
the world, and that wouldn't be possible in my native language. Second, I need
to improve my English, so writing and making videos in English will help me
practice.

## So, who am I?

I'm an introverted and ambitious person who wants to have a successful life. Of
course, "success" is subjective. Each of us has different goals, different
family contexts, different levels of ambition, and so on. For me, a successful
life means two things.

First, having enough money and health to take care of my family. Second,
reaching a point where I work to build something useful for myself or for the
world, not just to pay the bills. I want enough freedom to spend time on my hobbies without feeling guilty that I'm not
using that time to grow my career.

## My story

Where I grew up, most people had that old idea that only a few careers could give
you a comfortable life: lawyer, civil engineer, or doctor. But for some reason I
can't explain, ever since I was a kid, I imagined myself working with something
related to computers.

I spent a lot of time on the computer playing online games, mostly competitive
ones: MU, Ragnarok, Combat Arms, DDTank, World of Warcraft (on a pirate
server, because I didn't have money), Dota, Grand Chase, CS, League of
Legends... The list goes on.

The thing is, nobody around me worked with computers. So for a while, I
pictured myself as a civil engineer, or as a forensic expert for the Federal
Police.

Then, while I was still in high school, I enrolled in a technical course at
[Instituto Metrópole Digital](https://www.metropoledigital.ufrn.br/), the IT
institute of the Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte (UFRN). There I saw
programming for the first time, and my mind was blown. At that moment, I knew
this was what I wanted to do for the rest of my life, even without fully
understanding what "this" was.

That course was where it all started. Not long after, I landed my first job at
a company that was migrating a legacy desktop system, built in Delphi, to a web
application in Django, all running on SQL Server. I still remember
reading the entire Django documentation in a single day and working through
every tutorial I could find. One week later, I was delivering my first tasks
straight to production (there was no code review back then 😂).

Since then, I've worked at many different companies, and each one taught me
something new. After all these years, I feel it's time to share some of what
I've learned.

## Why this blog and channel?

Over the years, I've consumed a lot of content from other engineers and
creators. I've read countless articles and joined many communities. But I never
stopped to think about creating my own place.

That's one of my main goals here: to build a community. It might be the
smallest slice of the internet, but it's a place I can call mine. A place where
I can share knowledge, experiences, and mistakes, and where I can discuss ideas
and learn with anyone who wants to be part of it.

Talking to a camera is also completely new to me, so the channel is a great
opportunity to learn something different.

Here and on YouTube, I'll write and make videos about computer science,
software engineering, and my journey. I'll share my opinions and experiences,
and I'll always try to bring evidence to the discussion.
